## The VATUSA written exam

Your course has a written component on VATUSA's training system, alongside the
live sessions you will fly with an instructor.

**It is assigned when your training starts, not when you join the waitlist.**
Watch for it once a mentor has been assigned to you.

You will need to pass it before your certification is issued, but it does not
have to be the first thing you do — your instructor will tell you when to sit
it relative to your live sessions.
