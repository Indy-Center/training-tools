## The VATUSA Academy course

Your training requires you to complete a self study course on the VATUSA Academy, alongside the live sessions.

To ensure the content is fresh in your mind **it may not be assigned as soon as you join the waitlist.** You'll get a notification when it has been assigned.

Once the VATUSA Academy course is assigned, you have 30 days to complete it. It must be complete prior to your rating exam.
